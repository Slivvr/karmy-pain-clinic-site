About Us

One of Ontario's Oldest & Most Trusted Pain Clinics

Our team of highly experienced anaesthesiologists and pain physicians use the latest evidence-based treatments to help patients regain quality of life. Most of our treatments are covered by OHIP, making world-class pain care accessible to all Ontarians.

Our Treatments

Our Treatments & Procedures

Our physicians use advanced evidence-based techniques to deliver targeted, lasting pain relief.

Nerve Blocks

Precise injections that interrupt pain signal pathways, providing significant and lasting relief for nerve-related chronic pain conditions.

✓ OHIP Covered

Trigger Point Injections

Targeted injections into muscle knots and trigger points to release tension and relieve localized muscle pain.

✓ OHIP Covered

Epidural Injections

Corticosteroid injections into the epidural space to reduce inflammation and relieve back and leg pain.

✓ OHIP Covered

Botox for Migraines

FDA-approved Botox therapy to prevent chronic migraines, administered every 12 weeks by experienced specialists.

✗ Not OHIP Covered

Fluoroscopically Guided Lumbar Spine Injections

Image-guided injections using live X-ray (fluoroscopy) for precise delivery of medication to the lumbar spine, improving accuracy and outcomes.

✓ OHIP Covered 📍 Brampton location only

Radiofrequency Ablation for Lower Back

A minimally invasive procedure using heat generated by radio waves to disrupt nerve signals causing chronic lower back pain, providing long-lasting relief.

✓ OHIP Covered 📍 Brampton location only

Neurology & Headache Assessments

Comprehensive neurological evaluations and headache assessments conducted by our specialist neurologist to diagnose and develop personalized treatment plans.

✓ OHIP Covered

Tendon Sheath & Joint Cortisone Injections

Cortisone injections for inflamed tendons and joints, performed both landmark-based and with real-time ultrasound guidance for enhanced precision and safety.

✓ OHIP Covered
